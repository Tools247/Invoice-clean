About InvoiceClean

A simple tool for people who just need a good-looking invoice.

Why this exists

Most free invoice generators force you to create an account, add watermarks, limit downloads, or track everything you type. We wanted something better: open the page, fill in the details, download a clean PDF, and leave.

InvoiceClean is built for SaaS founders, indie hackers, freelancers, and small agencies who sell software or services and need professional invoices without the complexity of full accounting software.

What we care about

  • Speed – From blank page to PDF in under a minute.
  • Privacy – Everything runs in your browser. We never see your data.
  • Design quality – Invoices that look like they came from a modern company, not a 2012 template.
  • Zero friction – No signup, no email capture, no paywall.

Who it’s for

Anyone who needs to send a professional invoice occasionally or regularly and doesn’t want another subscription or account to manage. Especially useful if you already use Stripe, Paddle, or another billing system and just need a standalone PDF from time to time.

Free forever

The core generator will stay free. No hidden limits, no “upgrade to remove watermark.” If we ever add optional paid features later, they will be clearly separated and never required for basic use.
