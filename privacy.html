Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

Your invoice data never leaves your browser.
We do not store it, transmit it, or have any way to see it.

How the tool works

InvoiceClean is a client-side web application. All calculations, previews, and PDF generation happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. When you click “Download PDF,” the file is created on your device and saved to your computer. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

What we collect

We collect almost nothing:

  • No accounts – There is no signup, login, or user database.
  • No invoice content – Company names, client details, amounts, logos, and notes stay on your device.
  • Local storage only – A draft of your current invoice is saved in your browser’s localStorage so a page refresh doesn’t lose your work. This data never leaves your computer and can be cleared at any time.
  • Theme preference – Your light/dark mode choice is also stored locally.

Analytics & tracking

We currently use no third-party analytics that identify individual users. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future (for example, aggregate page-view counts), we will update this page and choose tools that do not track personal data or require cookies for identification.

Third-party libraries

The PDF feature uses open-source libraries loaded from a public CDN (jsPDF and html2canvas). These libraries run in your browser and do not send your data anywhere.

Cookies

We do not set any tracking cookies. The only storage we use is standard browser localStorage for drafts and theme preference.

Children’s privacy

This tool is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the tool after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
